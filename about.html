Project Reflection

Building this website across three milestones taught me how design decisions cascade through a project. Adding responsive behavior in Milestone 3 required revisiting every page — updating the navigation, wrapping tables, resizing images, and adjusting font sizes — which reinforced the value of planning for mobile from the start.

Researching SEO also showed me that good web development and good SEO are often the same thing: semantic markup, fast load times, accessible content, and clear navigation benefit both human users and search engine crawlers simultaneously.
